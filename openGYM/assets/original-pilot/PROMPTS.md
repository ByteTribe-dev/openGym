# Pilot prompt set

Mode: Codex built-in image generation. No existing Gym Visual or exercise-dataset image was supplied as an input or reference.

## Shared visual prompt

Create an original exercise-library animation sprite sheet for a mobile fitness app. Use a clean 3D educational fitness render on a near-black charcoal studio gradient, with an original gender-neutral athletic mannequin in matte charcoal training clothes, bright lime joint accents, generic unbranded equipment, soft studio lighting, a crisp full-body silhouette, consistent camera/equipment/scale across frames, and no text, numbers, logos, watermark, or border. Frames must read left-to-right across rows and remain crop-safe. Avoid copying existing exercise artwork, extra limbs, inconsistent equipment, or unsafe mechanics.

## Exercise-specific prompts

1. **Barbell back squat — six frames:** standing, controlled descent, bottom, and return. Bar across upper back; knees track over toes; feet planted; neutral spine.
2. **Flat barbell bench press — six frames:** arms extended, controlled lowering to lower chest, bottom, and press to extension. Stable contact points, vertical forearms near the bottom, controlled bar path.
3. **Conventional deadlift — six frames:** setup, floor pull, pass knees, upright lockout, controlled descent, floor. Bar over midfoot and close to legs; neutral braced spine; hips and shoulders rise together.
4. **Strict overhand pull-up — four frames:** dead hang, halfway, chin above bar, controlled return. Both hands grip one top bar in every frame; pronated grip; no kipping or leg swing.
5. **Alternating dumbbell curl — six frames:** both arms down, right elbow flexion, top, controlled return. Left arm remains still; neutral wrists; elbows close to torso; no body swing.

The pull-up was regenerated after the first draft failed continuity checks. Only the replacement sheet is retained in the project pilot.
